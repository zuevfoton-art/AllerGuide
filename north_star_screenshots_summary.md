## Technical Note

The mockup uses fixed-height phone containers (667px) with overflow:hidden on the scroll area. 
To reveal the buttons at the bottom of the Today phone, the overflow property was temporarily 
changed to "visible" using Chrome DevTools, allowing full visibility of all UI elements.
